About the hotel

Comfort, care, and celebrations under one roof.

The Sheesham Tree is a hotel, restaurant, and banquet destination at ISBT Commercial Scheme, Bhopal. The experience is built around hassle-free accommodation, sincere hospitality, clean air-conditioned rooms, pure-veg dining, secure premises, and event spaces made for memorable occasions.

Guests arrive for work trips, wedding functions, family visits, birthdays, kitty parties, and city travel. The redesigned experience presents the hotel as warm, trustworthy, and professionally managed, with every key service easy to discover and enquire about.

Bhopal's attractions

Explore the city from a convenient ISBT location.

The Sheesham Tree gives guests a comfortable base for visiting Bhopal's lakeside views, temples, heritage landmarks, and city routes.

Boat Club

A lively lakeside destination known for boating, evening views, and the beauty of Bhopal's water landscape. The old site notes Boat Club is about 11.5 kilometers from the hotel.

Birla Mandir

Also known as Laxmi Narayan Temple, this hilltop landmark is one of the city's notable places to visit.

VIP Road

One of Bhopal's most scenic city routes, known for lake views, evening drives, and a memorable sense of arrival.

Taj-ul-Masajid

A prominent historic mosque in Bhopal and one of the city's most recognizable architectural landmarks.
